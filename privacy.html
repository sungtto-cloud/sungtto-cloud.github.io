Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Nest MagicMirror is a personal, non-commercial home display integration created for private use.

Information Accessed

Nest MagicMirror may access Google Nest device information authorized by the owner through Google's Device Access API. This may include device status and events needed to display Nest information on the owner's private MagicMirror.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide functionality on the owner's personal MagicMirror display.

Data Sharing

Nest MagicMirror does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties.

Data Storage

Any credentials or configuration information used by the integration are maintained for the owner's personal use and are not made publicly available through this website.

Access and Revocation

The owner can revoke the application's access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the developer through the support contact associated with the application's Google OAuth configuration.
